KINGDOM TILES : RISE OF REALMS - v0.1 Early Access (Windows)
=============================================================

CACH CHAY
- Giai nen file zip ra mot thu muc bat ky, chay KingdomTiles.exe.
- Lan dau mo, Windows co the hien "Windows protected your PC" (vi game chua ky so).
  Bam "More info" (Thong tin them) -> "Run anyway" (Van chay).
- Can Windows 10/11 64-bit, card do hoa ho tro OpenGL 3.3.

CACH CHOI
- Menu chinh -> "Cach choi" de xem huong dan ngan.
- Phim tat: 1-3 chon manh dat, Enter ket thuc luot, Esc dong cua so, Space tam dung tran danh.

BAN LUU VA BAO LOI
- Ban luu va nhat ky loi nam o:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Kingdom Tiles - Rise Of Realms\
- Neu game loi hoac tu tat: Menu chinh -> "Gioi thieu" -> tab "Bao loi" -> "Mo thu muc nhat ky",
  gui file godot.log cho tac gia kem vai dong ke lai ban dang lam gi.

Day la ban choi thu som: luat choi va can bang con thay doi, ban luu co the khong dung duoc o phien ban sau.
